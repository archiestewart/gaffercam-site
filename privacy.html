Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

GafferCam is built to keep your picture on your Mac. Contact: gaffercamapp@gmail.com.

Your camera picture

The picture is processed only on your Mac. It is not uploaded, stored by us or used to train anything. The skin, face and depth analysis runs on your Mac and is used only to apply the effects you turned on. The app does not identify people.

Output goes only where you send it: to OBS through Syphon, or to a snapshot in your Pictures folder when you press Save Frame.

What the app sends over the network

  • At launch it loads a banner page, with scripts off and nothing stored.
  • At launch and every 6 hours it checks for a new version.
  • If you use a purchased license key, it contacts our license server when you activate it and from time to time for a subscription. It sends the key and a label for this computer, and the server sees your IP address and the time. Nothing about your picture is sent.

The first two come from GitHub Pages, which can see your IP address and the time of the request, like any website. The app sends no account, usage statistics or analytics of its own. Promo codes are checked on your Mac with no network.

Settings and license

Settings, styles and your license key stay on your Mac.

Purchases

When you buy Pro, Creem, our merchant of record, processes your payment and your details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details in order to send your key and help you. We do not see your full card number.

Support

If you write to us, we keep your message and address to answer you. Ask us at any time to delete them or the order details we hold, unless the law requires us to keep them.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics of its own. Its host can see your IP address.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
