Questions

Which apps can I use it with?
GafferCam sends its picture to OBS. From there, OBS's Virtual Camera can carry it to Zoom, Meet, Telegram and others. GafferCam has no virtual camera of its own yet.
Which Mac do I need?
A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or newer. Heavy effects such as bokeh use more power and may warm up a fanless Mac.
Does it send my video anywhere?
No. The picture is processed on your Mac. At launch the app loads a banner and checks for a new version from our site, which sees your IP address like any website. See the privacy policy.
Does it change who you look like?
No. GafferCam is a color and light tool with cosmetic effects you control. It does not swap faces, imitate other people or recognize anyone.
What happens if I cancel?
Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then the app goes on as Free, with your settings kept. The forever license does not expire and includes updates within its major version.
